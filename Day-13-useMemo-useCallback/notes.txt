1.useMemo — Remember a result
Problem: A calculation runs again every time a component renders, even when its inputs haven't changed.
Solution: useMemo remembers the previous result and recalculates only when its dependencies change.
Example from NoteSpace: remembering the filtered notes until notes or search changes.
2. React.memo — Skip an unnecessary child render
Problem: A parent renders again, causing its child to render again even though the child's props haven't changed.
Solution: React.memo lets React skip rendering that child when its props are unchanged.
3. useCallback — Remember a function
Problem: A function declared inside a parent component gets a new reference on every render. This can make a React.memo child render again.
Solution: useCallback preserves the function reference while its dependencies remain unchanged.
